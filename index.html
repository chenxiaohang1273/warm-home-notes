Read the full FAQ ↗
Will my candle jar fit?

Measure its actual height and widest diameter, then check the lamp’s usable platform and required operating clearance. Wax weight alone does not establish fit.

Does flameless mean risk-free?

No. A warmer still has heated parts, an electrical supply and potentially hot glass and wax. Follow both sets of manufacturer instructions.

Which replacement bulb should I use?

Use the exact specification in the lamp manual. A matching socket does not establish compatibility, and higher wattage is not a shortcut to faster warming.

Can I leave it on while sleeping?

Our conservative recommendation is to switch it off before sleeping or leaving. A timer is a convenience, not supervision.

Will warming make every candle last longer?

Wax is not consumed in the same way as burning, but useful fragrance can diminish. We do not claim a fixed lifetime increase.

Are these product reviews or affiliate recommendations?

These are educational buying and care guides. This edition includes no hands-on test claims, product rankings or affiliate shopping links.
