What matters more than the photo?

A beautiful lamp may not fit the candle jar you already own. Before comparing designs, check these practical details.

01

Fit & clearance

Check the maximum jar height and the distance between the warming bulb and the candle surface. Confirm the base can support your jar securely.

02

Controls

Look for clear information about brightness adjustment, timer options and any replacement bulb requirements. Features vary by model.

03

Materials & stability

Consider the base weight, cable routing, easy-to-clean surfaces and whether the lamp stays stable on a level table.

Buying guide

Five questions to ask before ordering

1. Will my candle jar fit?

Measure your candle jar's height and width. Compare them with the seller's published clearance and base dimensions. Do not rely on photos alone.

2. Is the bulb specified?

Check the correct bulb type and wattage in the product instructions. Never substitute a higher-wattage bulb unless the manufacturer explicitly allows it.

3. Which controls will I actually use?

Compare models with a timer, dimmer, both, or neither. Favor a straightforward control layout over extra features you don't need.

4. Where will it go?

Plan for a steady, heat-resistant surface, accessible power outlet and space away from curtains, children and pets, following the manufacturer's guidance.

5. Can I find care instructions?

Read the manual for operation, cleaning and bulb replacement. Do not leave the lamp operating unattended if the manufacturer instructs otherwise.

6. What does the seller actually guarantee?

Compare return terms, warranty details and included accessories. Only treat claims backed by the individual listing as verified.
