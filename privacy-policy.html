Privacy Policy — Current Static Website

A privacy policy draft describing this static site’s current features, hosting-related request data and changes needed before adding tracking or forms.

Prepared October 12, 2026

Scope and current status

This is a privacy policy draft for Warm Home Notes at https://www.whychooseme.me. It describes the supplied static website code as of October 12, 2026. The owner must verify the live hosting configuration and applicable requirements before publication or promotional use.

The website owner’s legal identity and a working privacy contact channel have not yet been supplied. Our contact page reflects that limitation. This draft does not claim comprehensive compliance with every privacy law.

Features provided by this version

The source code does not include analytics, advertising conversion scripts, Meta Pixel, affiliate tracking, accounts, a newsletter service or a contact form. There are no embedded third-party videos, remotely hosted fonts or social widgets. Site illustrations, styles and scripts are served with the site.

The mobile menu and FAQ sections operate in your browser. Checklist selections are for on-screen reference; the site does not send them to a server or intentionally persist them in cookies or browser storage. A browser may retain page state or cache files under its own settings.

Hosting and technical request data

The site is intended to be served through Cloudflare Pages. Serving and protecting web requests can involve technical information such as IP addresses, request URLs, timestamps and browser-related data. Hosting data processing, retention and any security mechanisms depend on the actual service configuration and provider practices.

Cloudflare describes its handling of End Users’ technical data in its Privacy Policy. Its role is separate from the small browser interactions described above. This website cannot promise that no technical data is processed simply because its source contains no analytics script.

The source does not intentionally set tracking cookies. Any cookies or scripts added by hosting settings, security services or later changes must be checked on the deployed domain and reflected here. The site owner has not supplied a verified access-log retention period.

Links to other websites

Articles may link to manufacturer guidance and other primary sources. When you follow a link, the destination operates under its own practices and policies. We do not control those websites. A reference link does not establish an affiliate relationship or transfer a checklist selection.

Contact information and future changes

There is currently no site-operated channel collecting messages or email addresses. If an email contact or form is added, this policy should explain the information received, purpose, access, retention and available requests. Do not assume a future contact service is already operational.

Before enabling analytics, advertising tags, affiliate tracking, email marketing or third-party embeds, identify the actual tools and data flows. Update this policy and implement any consent or privacy controls required for that use and audience. A policy notice alone is not a substitute for required consent management.

Requests and updates

Privacy rights and required disclosures may depend on where readers and the operator are located and what processing actually occurs. The owner should provide an effective contact route and obtain suitable advice where needed. This draft does not invent a statutory response period or an organization-wide compliance claim.

Update the stated date when the site's relevant processing changes. Before relying on this draft, verify the production network requests, cookies, Cloudflare settings and any connected services.
