Content questions and corrections

Once a working channel is available, include the page title or URL, the passage in question and a clear explanation of the issue. A link to a manufacturer manual or another relevant primary source can help us understand a proposed correction.

For a broken link, identify where you found it and the destination you expected. For a layout or accessibility issue, describe the device, browser and action that caused the problem. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Product and purchase support

Warm Home Notes provides educational information. It is not a retailer or a manufacturer support service. Contact the seller or manufacturer for orders, returns, warranty claims, model-specific compatibility and repairs.

If a lamp has damaged electrical parts or behaves abnormally, stop using it and seek appropriate manufacturer or qualified assistance. We cannot diagnose or authorize repairs through this website.

Business inquiries

After a contact channel is configured, relevant home-content inquiries may be sent there. This page does not imply any existing commercial partnership or promise a response time. Proposed product claims or marketing relationships require separate verification before use.
